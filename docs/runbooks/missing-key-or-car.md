# Missing key or car

**Owner:** business — _to be named_; admin on duty resolves.

## Steps (supervisor)
1. Find the ticket and open it. Check the recorded zone/spot, key hook, photos, the valet who parked it and
   the timeline.
2. **Safety hold** (blocking retrieval and handover), with the reason. This stops dispatch and stops any
   handover until the hold is cleared.
3. **Open incident** — category "Missing vehicle" or "Key issue" — and describe what was checked.
4. Search physically: recorded spot, neighbouring zones, key board around the recorded hook.
5. Add notes to the incident as facts come in (who checked what, when).
6. When found: **Clear hold** with the reason. If the car or key was somewhere other than recorded, use
   **Relocate car**: the chosen valet accepts the move in their app, moves the car and records the new zone, spot
   and key hook. If their device is not working, see [whatsapp-outage.md](whatsapp-outage.md) section 2. The car's recorded
   location changes only when the move is completed.

## Admin
- Resolves the incident with the outcome. If the vehicle cannot be found, escalate to the venue and the owner's
  insurer per the business owner's policy (_to be written by the business owner_).
