# Stuck accepted task

**Owner:** business — _to be named_.

## Signs
- Attention item "Accepted retrieval overdue"; the request shows "Accepted" with a long wait.

## Steps (supervisor)
1. Contact the valet holding the task by radio or phone. Most delays are real (traffic in the lot, a blocked car).
2. If the valet still has the car and key: let them finish. Do nothing in the console.
3. If the valet cannot continue but is reachable: **Transfer custody** to another available valet, stating
   where the car and the key are now. The receiving valet must confirm in WhatsApp before custody moves.
4. If the guest no longer wants the car: **Withdraw with custody resolution** — record the zone, spot
   and key hook the car and key were returned to. The ticket stays open and the car stays parked.
5. If the valet is unreachable and the car or key location is unknown: treat it as
   [missing-key-or-car.md](missing-key-or-car.md).

## Never
- Never reassign an accepted task without a custody record. There is no automatic reassignment.
