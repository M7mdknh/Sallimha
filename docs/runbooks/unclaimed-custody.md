# Unclaimed vehicle at event end

**Owner:** business — _to be named_; manager approves and releases.

## Steps
1. **Supervisor:** for each car still parked when the event ends, open the ticket and **Propose unclaimed**,
   naming the custodian (for example venue security) and the plan (overnight, call the guest in the morning).
2. **Manager:** review the proposal and **Approve unclaimed**. The ticket becomes Unclaimed and the key stays on
   its venue key hook. The hook remains occupied for later events at that venue until released.
3. The event can now close: closing is refused while cars are in custody without an approved carryover case,
   or while shifts are open.
4. **Carryover** (managers) lists every carried-over car and key across events.
5. When the owner collects the car: verify identity in person, then **Release key and car** with the method
   ("collected by the verified owner", "moved to storage" or other) and a note. Only then is the hook freed.

## Never
- Never mark a car as delivered to close an event.
