## Bugs found by these checks and fixed
- Intake photos were not the primary action when a supervisor records for a valet without a device; they now are
  until four photos (or an exception) exist — test written red first.
- The simulator's outage switch offered "simulate outage" before it knew the provider's state (could leave the
  provider down); it now appears only after the state is read.
- Valet "recent work" times were cut off ("1:14:"); hour:minute formatting fixed.
- Ticket-panel action forms could have been remounted on every refresh (nested component); rendered as a function.

## Not verified here
- Real WhatsApp delivery, templates and the 24-hour window: **BLOCKED** until staging (docs/staging/whatsapp-onboarding.md).
- Real phones and tablets: layouts were checked in Chromium device emulation only; camera capture on iOS/Android
  devices, screen lock behaviour and venue Wi-Fi are to be tried in the pilot rehearsal.
- Load against declared targets, backup restore on the provider and rollback: staging items, **NOT RUN**.
